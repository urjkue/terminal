Beta Terminal

A finance terminal built from scratch, combining my passion for finance and programming. Designed to analyze companies, retrieve market information, and provide AI-powered insights.

Watch Demo

Project Overview

Beta Terminal is a self-hosted finance platform that gathers company information, market data, news, and financial metrics into a terminal-style interface.


The application uses AI to analyze retrieved data and provide insights directly within the terminal. News can also be browsed from sources such as Yahoo Finance and France 24.


The name Beta comes from both finance (beta risk measurement) and my interest in Betta fish.

Features

Core functionality currently available in the beta version.

📈 Company Analysis

Retrieve financial data and company information instantly.

🤖 AI Insights

Local AI model analyzes retrieved information and generates summaries.

📰 News Aggregation

Browse financial news from multiple sources within the terminal.

🔒 Self Hosted

Runs entirely on personal infrastructure and local AI models.
